# 1962 Ritus servandus and De defectibus

Study transcriptions, Latin only, of the 1962 typical edition of the Roman Missal. They are not a scan. Check them against the typica before treating them as a critical text.

- `Ritus_servandus_1962.txt` — *Ritus servandus in celebratione Missae*, titles I–XIV.
- `De_defectibus_1962.txt` — *De defectibus in celebratione Missae occurrentibus*, which follows title XIV in the same book.

Sources:

- The 1962 typica scan: <https://archive.org/details/missale_romanum-1962> and the same PDF at <https://media.churchmusicassociation.org/pdf/missale62.pdf>. *De oratione* is title V, typica page LVII.
- The Latin columns of <https://www.ceremoniaire.net/pastorale1950/docs/ritus_servandus_1962_1.html>, `_2.html`, `_3.html`, and <https://www.ceremoniaire.net/pastorale1950/docs/de_defectibus_1962.html>. The site’s 2007 French translation and notes were not copied.
- The 1960 Code of Rubrics, AAS 52: <https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-52-1960-ocr.pdf>.

IntraText LAT0820 (<https://www.intratext.com/IXT/LAT0820/_PC.HTM>) is a pre-1962 edition. It is useful for comparison only.

Title V of the Ritus file was read against the CMAA scan, page LVII. The sentence *Si plures orationes sint dicendae, idem in eis, in voce, extensione manuum, et capitis inclinatione, quod supra dictum est, observatur* is on that page, and n. 1 says *Oremus* before the oration. The scan’s OCR breaks *dicendae* across a line and misreads some letters.
