[Oratio]
O God, who didst will that blessed Philippine, a virgin renowned for the ardour of her apostolic charity and her assiduous prayer, should proclaim Thy glory among the peoples of North America: grant us, through her intercession, that we too may remain steadfast in Thy service, and obtain everlasting glory.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio94]
Philippine Rosalie Duchesne was born at Grenoble in France in the year 1769, and from her infancy was devoted to the religious life. She entered among the nuns of the Visitation, but when the monastery was dissolved by the French Revolution, she consecrated herself in the year 1804 to the Society of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, newly founded by Blessed Madeleine Sophie Barat. In the year 1818 she sailed for North America; there, across the Mississippi River in the town of St. Charles, she founded the first school of her Society in the New World, and spent many years afterward in Missouri and Louisiana in teaching and evangelizing. In her seventy-second year she betook herself to the Potawatomi people in Kansas, and there gave herself so assiduously to prayer that she was called by the natives themselves "the Woman Who Prays Always." On the eighteenth day of November in the year 1852 she rested in the Lord at St. Charles. Pius XII enrolled her among the Blessed, and John Paul II, in the year 1988, among the Saints of the Virgins.
