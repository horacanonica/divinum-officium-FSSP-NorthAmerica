[Officium]
St. John Mary Vianney, Confessor

[Rank]
St. John Mary Vianney, Confessor, Patron of the Chapel;;Duplex I classis;;6;;ex C5

[Oratio]
Almighty and merciful God, who didst make Saint John Mary wonderful in his pastoral zeal and the unceasing ardour of his prayer and penance: grant, we beseech Thee, that by his example and intercession we may be able to win the souls of our brethren for Christ, and with them attain to everlasting glory.
