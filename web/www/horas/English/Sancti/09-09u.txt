[Oratio]
O God, who, in order to call enslaved Africans to the knowledge of Thy Name, didst strengthen Blessed Peter with wonderful charity and patience in their service: grant us by his intercession; that, seeking the things of Jesus Christ, we may love our neighbours in deed and in truth.
$Per eumdem

[Lectio94]
Peter Claver, born in Catalonia, gave himself to the Society of Jesus while yet a youth of most blameless life. While he was studying philosophy, he was taught the mysteries of higher virtue by Saint Alphonsus Rodriguez. Raised to the priesthood at Cartagena in South America, he wholly devoted his life by solemn pledge to the enslaved Africans brought thither by a shameful trade. In fostering, nourishing, tending, instructing them in the Christian faith, and baptising them, it would be well-nigh impossible to say with how much patience and charity he laboured for more than forty years, being also illustrious through the gift of healing which God bestowed upon him. After his daily toils, he gave himself to prayer until far into the night, and to harsh penance, for which he had an insatiable desire. He had reached the age of seventy and one, when, overcome by bodily weakness, he was compelled to lie ill in his chamber for four years; and at length, upon the birthday of the Mother of God, whom he had venerated with most tender affection, he departed to the saints. Leo XIII enrolled him among the Saints and appointed him the special heavenly patron of the sacred missions to Africans.
