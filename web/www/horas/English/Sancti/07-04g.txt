[Officium]
Our Lady, Refuge of Sinners

[Rank]
Our Lady, Refuge of Sinners;;Duplex II classis;;5;;vide C11

[Oratio]
O almighty and merciful God, who hast placed in the Blessed Virgin Mary ever a refuge and help for sinners: grant that, sheltered beneath her protection, and loosed from all our offences, we may obtain the happy effect of Thy mercy.
$Per Dominum
