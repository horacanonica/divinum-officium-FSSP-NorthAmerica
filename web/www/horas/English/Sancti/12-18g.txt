[Oratio]
O God, who wast pleased that Thy Word, at the announcement of an Angel, should take flesh in the womb of the Blessed Virgin Mary: grant to Thy suppliants; that we who believe her to be truly the Mother of God, may be aided by her intercession with Thee.
