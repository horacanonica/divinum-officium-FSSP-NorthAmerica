How to put these EPUBs on your device

Each file is a full year of the Latin Office for one calendar. Download the year you need from the list above.

Kindle

  1. Find your Kindle’s Send-to-Kindle email (Amazon account → Content & Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings).
  2. Email the .epub to that address from an approved sender, or use Amazon’s Send to Kindle app or web uploader. If you email the file, put Convert in the subject.
  3. Open it from your Kindle library once it appears. The Kindle needs Wi-Fi for the file to arrive.

Apple (iPhone and iPad)

Same steps for both.

  1. Tap the download, then tap Download.
  2. In the Files app, open Downloads and tap the .epub.
  3. Tap Share, then Books (Apple Books), or any EPUB reader you prefer.

Android (phone and tablet)

Same steps for both.

  1. Tap the download. The file is saved in Downloads.
  2. Open it with an EPUB reader (Google Play Books, Lithium, Moon+ Reader, or another).
  3. In Play Books: Library → + → Upload, then choose the file.

Computer

Open the .epub in any EPUB reader (Apple Books on a Mac, Calibre, Adobe Digital Editions, Edge, or another).

Other e-readers (Kobo, Nook, PocketBook, and others)

Transfer the EPUB over USB, or with that device’s companion app. Search for “[your device] add EPUB” for the exact steps. There are too many models to list here.

Tips

  • One year per calendar. Pick your parish, or the closest general calendar, for the right propers.
  • Once the file is downloaded, it works offline. The files themselves do not need a login.
